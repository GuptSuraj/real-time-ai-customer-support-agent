Guide: Monitor display and connectivity problems

No image on the monitor

Check that the monitor has power and that the input source matches the connected cable. Confirm the HDMI or DisplayPort cable is seated securely, and test another supported port or cable when available. Device and resolution support vary by model.

Wrong resolution or refresh rate

Use the computer's display settings to choose a mode supported by the monitor, cable and computer. Do not promise a specific refresh rate unless it is listed in that product's official specifications.

When to contact support

For cracks, electrical faults or persistent failure, stop troubleshooting and offer a support ticket with the signed-in customer's order information. Warranty and return eligibility require product-specific verification.
