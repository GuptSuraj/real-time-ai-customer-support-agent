Guide: Troubleshooting wireless headphones

Bluetooth connection problems

Confirm that the headphones are charged and in pairing mode according to their own manual. On the phone or computer, remove a stale saved Bluetooth pairing if appropriate, then pair again. Nearby devices may auto-connect and prevent a new connection. Exact steps vary by model.

Audio only on one side or distorted sound

Check the playback balance and output device, try a different app, and test a second source device if available. If the product uses removable ear tips, ensure they are fitted correctly. Avoid opening the device or attempting internal electrical repairs.
