Guide: USB-C chargers and charging problems

Check compatibility first

USB-C is a connector type, not a guarantee that every charger delivers the power a device expects. Compare the device's charging requirements, cable capability and the charger's documented output. Mira must not invent wattage or USB Power Delivery support for a specific Northstar product.

If charging is slow

Try a known-good compatible cable and outlet, examine the connector for visible obstruction without inserting metal objects, and check whether the device reports a charging limitation. Charge speed may change with device temperature and battery level.

If a charger is damaged

Stop using equipment with a frayed cable, exposed conductor, overheating, smoke, or a visibly damaged plug. Offer a support ticket for human review. Never suggest opening a mains-powered charger.
