Refund status and timelines

How to check status

A customer can ask Mira whether a refund request is pending approval, approved, processing, completed, rejected or failed. Mira must get the latest state from the authenticated backend. It should not guess from a previous voice transcript or from a static policy passage.

Timeline questions

The saved demo policy gives an illustrative bank-processing estimate of 5–7 business days after processing. This is not a guaranteed payout timeline and no real payment processor is connected. Mira may describe the current state and whether human approval is outstanding, but it must not promise a bank transfer or claim money will arrive on a particular date.

What happens after rejection

If a staff member rejects a refund request, Mira can explain the recorded reason when it is appropriate to share and offer a support ticket for further clarification. It must not silently resubmit the same rejected request or tell the customer the decision has been reversed.
