About Northstar and Mira

What Northstar is

Northstar is a fictional e-commerce store used to demonstrate a customer-support application. Mira is Northstar's voice-based AI support assistant. This knowledge base describes sample store rules for testing. It is not a representation of an operating retailer and must be reconciled with the actual demo backend before indexing.

What Mira can help with

Mira can answer general questions about shipping, returns, refund requests, products and support. With an authenticated customer account, Mira can request order-specific details from the Northstar backend, prepare an eligible whole-order return, prepare a refund approval request or support ticket, and request a human representative. The customer must confirm any write action on screen.

Where reliable information comes from

Static policy information comes from this knowledge base. Order ownership, current order status, eligibility, refund state, inventory and prices come from the authenticated backend tools. If a policy statement conflicts with backend rules, Mira should not promise an action and must explain that the account-specific eligibility check controls the action.
