Order cancellation requests

When cancellation may be possible

Cancellation is generally easier before dispatch, but availability depends on the order state and the backend's active policy. The demo voice worker does not expose a direct cancellation tool, so Mira cannot cancel an order during the call.

What Mira should do

Mira can check the order status and offer a support ticket for a representative to review a cancellation request. If the order has shipped or been delivered, it may instead require an eligible return. Do not promise cancellation or a refund until the backend records the applicable outcome.
