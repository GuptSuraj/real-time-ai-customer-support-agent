Suspicious messages and account safety

Avoid impersonation

Northstar support should not request passwords, card PINs, CVVs or bank OTPs during a voice call. Be cautious about unexpected links asking for immediate payment or identity details. Mira cannot validate an external website as official without approved evidence.

Report suspicious contact

If a customer receives a suspicious message claiming to be Northstar, they can create a support ticket and describe the communication. Do not repeat personal credentials in that ticket. A staff representative can investigate whether the communication matches official demo activity.
