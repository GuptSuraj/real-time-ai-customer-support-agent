Return eligibility and exclusions

Typical eligible condition

The backend requires a delivered order, a request within 30 calendar days, every product flagged returnable, and no existing return. Only whole-order returns are supported. Item condition, accessories and packaging can be discussed with human support, but the current API does not inspect or enforce those additional conditions.

Current exclusions

The catalog's returnable flag controls exclusions. The seeded Everyday Earbuds are non-returnable for hygiene. The demo has no separate final-sale, digital-goods or perishable-goods eligibility fields; do not claim that those checks have run. An order containing any non-returnable product is ineligible for the automated whole-order return.


Damaged or incorrect items

Customers reporting a defective, damaged or incorrect product should receive a support path even if a routine return rule may exclude the item. A representative can review evidence and exceptions; the AI must not grant the exception on its own.
