Shipping estimates

Standard delivery

For the synthetic Northstar demo policy, standard delivery is estimated at 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. The estimate is illustrative and is not a guaranteed arrival date. Weekends, holidays, courier disruptions and delivery location may change actual timing.

Express delivery

The current demo does not implement express shipping selection, express eligibility, or a shipping-upgrade tool. No express delivery estimate is published. Mira can offer a confirmed ticket for human review, without promising an upgrade.

Tracking or delays

For questions such as "Where is my package?", Mira should query the authenticated backend for the latest shipment state. The general delivery estimate is not a substitute for a live tracking update. If a shipment appears delayed, Mira can offer a support ticket rather than inventing a courier scan or delivery date.
