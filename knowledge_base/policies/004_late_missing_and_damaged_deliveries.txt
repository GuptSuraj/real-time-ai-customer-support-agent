Late, missing and damaged deliveries

Late delivery

If a shipment is later than the estimated timeframe, the customer can ask Mira to check the order status. If the backend has no current tracking event or a dispute requires investigation, Mira can offer a support ticket. No refund or compensation should be promised simply because the estimate was missed.

Marked delivered but not received

Ask the customer to check the provided delivery location and whether another authorized household member accepted the parcel. If it still cannot be found, offer a support ticket so a representative can investigate. Do not ask the customer to publish an address or identity document in a public channel.

Damaged parcel

If an item or package arrives damaged, encourage the customer to keep the packaging and describe the problem. A representative may ask for photos in the ticket. A potential damaged-item exception requires case review and must not be treated as automatic return or refund approval.
