Support tickets and follow-up requests

Creating a ticket

Mira can prepare a support ticket or callback request for an authenticated customer. Before creating it, the app shows the proposal and requires an expiring verification challenge and explicit on-screen confirmation. Repeated confirmations should return the existing result rather than creating duplicate tickets.

Ticket information

A support ticket may contain a problem summary, the associated order where relevant, a status, assignment and notes. The customer can review their own tickets. Staff can assign, update and resolve tickets according to role permissions.

When to use a ticket

Tickets are suitable when a return or cancellation requires an exception, a delivery is missing, an address change needs review, or Mira lacks enough verified information. Creating a ticket does not guarantee the requested commercial outcome.
