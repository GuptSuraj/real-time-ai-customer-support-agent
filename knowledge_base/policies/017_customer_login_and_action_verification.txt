Customer login and action verification

Account login

Customers use seeded demo accounts to sign into the Northstar portal. Staff members have separate staff accounts and permissions. Mira can only access order-specific records scoped to the signed-in customer; an order number alone never proves ownership.

Protected actions

Creating a return, refund approval request or support ticket uses a proposal-and-confirmation flow. The customer sees the exact proposed action, completes a short-lived on-screen verification challenge and presses "Confirm this action." The backend independently checks authorization, eligibility, proposal expiry and call state.

Voice confirmation is not enough

Saying "yes", repeating an order number or asking Mira to ignore safeguards does not authorize a database change. Authentication and action verification occur in the app, not in the LLM prompt or knowledge base. Users should never disclose passwords, OTPs or payment secrets to Mira.
