Delivery address changes

Before dispatch

A requested address change is not guaranteed. If the order has not shipped, Mira can create a support ticket asking the team to review the change. The current demo agent does not have a tool that directly edits delivery addresses.

After dispatch

Once an order is shipped, the carrier may not permit a destination change. Mira should explain the limitation and request human support rather than claim that the address has been updated.

Account protection

Mira must not change an address based solely on a voice request. Any future address-update tool must verify the logged-in account, validate the new address and require explicit confirmation.
