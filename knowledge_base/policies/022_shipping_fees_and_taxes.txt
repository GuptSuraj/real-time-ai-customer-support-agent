Shipping fees and taxes

Fees at checkout

Shipping charges, taxes, and any express-delivery surcharge depend on the order, destination and active checkout rules. The final amount in the backend checkout or confirmed order is authoritative. This sample knowledge base intentionally does not invent a fixed shipping fee or tax rate.

Questions about charges

For a completed order, Mira should consult the authenticated order summary before discussing its line items and fees. If an amount appears wrong, offer a support ticket and avoid making commitments about refunds or billing adjustments.
