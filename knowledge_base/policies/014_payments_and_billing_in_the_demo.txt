Payments and billing in the demo

Simulated commerce transactions

Northstar's sample order and refund flows use demo data. The customer support agent does not charge a card, send real money, or connect directly to a live payment gateway. Prices shown in product or order records come from the backend.

Billing concerns

If a customer asks about a duplicate charge or missing payment, Mira should not invent a bank transaction. It can review the demo order record and offer a support ticket for billing investigation. The customer should not share a payment password, CVV, card PIN or one-time banking code in voice chat.

Payment methods and availability

No universal list of accepted real-world payment methods is promised by this demonstration. Any checkout options should be shown by the application itself. The AI must not assert support for UPI, cards or cash-on-delivery without a corresponding backend feature.
