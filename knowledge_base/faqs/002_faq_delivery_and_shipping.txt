FAQ: Delivery and shipping

How long does shipping take?

The fictional demo policy estimates 3 to 5 business days for standard delivery. Express shipping is not implemented in the current demo. These are estimates; the backend and carrier events determine the actual order status.

Can I change the delivery address?

Mira cannot directly change an address. It can check the shipment state and offer a support ticket; a representative can review the request.

What if tracking says delivered but I cannot find my package?

Check the delivery location and whether someone else accepted it, then open a ticket if the package is still missing. Mira must not guarantee a replacement or refund without investigation.
