FAQ: Refunds

Can Mira refund me immediately?

No. Mira can prepare a refund request, but the customer must verify and confirm it on screen, and every request requires a human representative's approval. A return marked received by staff is required before a refund request; an existing refund cannot be duplicated. No actual money moves in this demo.

Where is my refund?

After account authentication, Mira can query the current backend status: pending approval, approved, processing, completed, rejected or a seeded historical failed refund. Static documents cannot confirm an individual refund status.

When will the refund reach my bank?

This demonstration uses simulated refund processing and has no guaranteed payout date or real payment provider. A simulated completed status does not prove that funds arrived at a bank.
