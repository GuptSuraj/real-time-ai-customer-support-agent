FAQ: Account protection and language

Why do I need to confirm an action on screen?

An AI-spoken answer is not a secure authorization method. The demo uses account-scoped data, an expiring on-screen verification challenge and explicit confirmation before creating returns, refund requests or support tickets.

Can I speak Hindi?

The customer portal offers English and Hindi language selection. Actual speech accuracy depends on the connected realtime model and must be validated through live audio testing.

Do I need to share my password with Mira?

No. Never speak or paste account passwords, payment PINs, CVVs or banking OTPs in a support conversation.
