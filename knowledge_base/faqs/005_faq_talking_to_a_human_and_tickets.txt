FAQ: Talking to a human and tickets

Can I speak to a person?

Yes. Ask Mira to connect a representative. The system queues an escalation, and an available staff member may claim the call. Mira stops speaking when the human takes over.

What if nobody answers?

After roughly 60 seconds without a successful takeover, the app offers a callback ticket. The customer must verify and confirm that request on screen.

Can I see my existing support tickets?

Yes. Signed-in customers can review their own tickets and previous call information from the customer portal.
